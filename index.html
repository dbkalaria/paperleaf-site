Common questions

Where are my scans stored?

In Paperleaf on your iPhone. Paperleaf never uploads your scans. If iCloud Backup is on, Apple's backup of your iPhone includes them.

How do I back up my documents?

Settings > Storage > Export all documents writes one searchable PDF per document to a folder in Files.

I bought Paperleaf on another iPhone.

Settings > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Family members get the unlock through Family Sharing.

Is there a page limit?

No. Free documents can have any number of pages, and text recognition and QR are free too. The one-time unlock adds Merge, Folders, the Face ID lock, Fill & Sign and more.

Text recognition missed something.

Handwriting is harder to read. Adjust the page or rescan it; changing the language in Settings offers "Re-read text" for older documents.
