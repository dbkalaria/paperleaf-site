Paperleaf

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. This policy matches About > Privacy policy in the app.

Paperleaf never uploads your scans. Documents, recognized text, saved codes and signatures are kept in Paperleaf on your iPhone.

Paperleaf has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect any data.

The camera is used only while you scan. Face ID is handled by iOS; Paperleaf never sees your face data.

Save to Photos only adds the QR codes you create; Paperleaf never reads your photo library.

The only connection Paperleaf makes is to the App Store, for the one-time purchase.

If iCloud Backup is on for this iPhone, Apple's backups include Paperleaf's files, as for any app.

With Show in Spotlight on, iOS keeps a search index of your document names and text so Spotlight can find them, and the Paperleaf widget shows your newest scan. While the Face ID lock is on, Paperleaf removes both.

With Auto-export on, Paperleaf saves a PDF of each document to the folder you chose in Files. If that folder is in iCloud Drive or another service, that service syncs the files, not Paperleaf.

Reminders are notifications scheduled on this iPhone. Translate works on this iPhone too; the first time you use a language, iOS downloads it from Apple. Your text is not sent.

Children

Paperleaf collects no data from anyone, including children.
